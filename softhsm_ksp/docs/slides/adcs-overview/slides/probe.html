Backend-agnostic by probe, not by porting

The provider always loaded whatever module a path pointed at — and then advertised a fixed list compiled into the DLL, describing one token. That was never a porting job; it was a correctness bug.

What the probe does

  • C_GetMechanismList once at initialisation
  • EnumAlgorithms answers the intersection of mapped and present
  • Generation and use are checked separately
  • A refused probe costs nothing — behaviour reverts to permissive
  • An implausible mechanism count abandons the probe rather than truncating it

What a probe cannot answer

Advertisement is not capability. SoftHSM2 advertises CKM_RSA_PKCS_OAEP and accepts only SHA-1, because the restriction lives inside the mechanism parameters. The probe is mechanism-granular; some limits are finer.

Strictness is not capability. Whether a token enforces CKA_WRAP is invisible to every query. So p11_qualify runs the requirements instead of reading them.
