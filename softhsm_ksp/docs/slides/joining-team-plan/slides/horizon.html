The first ninety days, and what “finalized” has to mean

Phrased against the header's arrival rather than a calendar, because the one thing that sets the schedule is the one thing nobody here controls.

First 30 days

Nothing depends on the DLL

  • Header escalation open; fallback decided
  • Certificate purchase under way
  • HSM qualified by p11_qualify; the authentication model known by measurement
  • DACL risk accepted or rejected, in writing
  • A4 closed; the Microsoft-documentation backlog retired
  • Pilot runbook written and reviewed by the CA operator

Days 30–60, from the machine's arrival

Gates B and C

  • The DLL builds; the signing question answered in the first hour
  • Registered; certutil lists it; the table layout confirmed
  • 190 tests executed and every failure classified
  • The DPAPI credential path proven, if the token needs a PIN
  • The CI Windows job compiles and links the real DLL

Days 60–90

Gate E, then the decision

  • E1–E7 twice on a throwaway CA, renewal included
  • Restart and reboot survived
  • Chains verified end to end, not merely API success
  • Test CA destroyed
  • A go or no-go recommendation with the evidence attached

Finalized means one sentence

A signed provider, on a certified HSM, running a certification authority that has issued, revoked and renewed — with the DACL limitation accepted in writing and the test CA destroyed. Anything short of renewal is not a finished CA; anything short of the signature is not a shippable provider.

Read every duration as relative: this project's effort estimates were wrong on 4 of the 4 rows it has ever closed · 10 / 10

Close on the one-sentence definition and leave it on screen. The three things most likely to be quietly dropped from a declaration of done are the signature, the renewal and the written risk acceptance — each is someone else's responsibility, which is exactly why each gets forgotten. Ask for the go or no-go recommendation as a deliverable, not as an opinion: a no-go with evidence at day 90 is a successful outcome for this plan.
