Procurement

The code-signing certificate has the longest lead time of anything here, and if Windows refuses unsigned providers it gates all testing, not just release.

Qualify the token

p11_qualify needs no Windows and no KSP build. It settles the authentication question by measurement and may close Phase C outright.

The DACL decision

Whether a CA key with no Windows ACL is acceptable. If the answer is no, everything above is wasted — so ask it before the machine arrives.

Three independent sources have already been checked for the header: a WDK-installed runner, the Windows SDK, and mingw-w64 · 3 / 10

The single most important message of the deck. The serial chain cannot be compressed by adding engineers — it is gated on an artefact nobody here has been able to obtain. What adding people does buy is the three parallel lanes, and two of those can retire risk before the machine is even procured. If the DACL answer comes back no, the project stops, so put that question in front of the CA's owner in week one rather than after the pilot.
